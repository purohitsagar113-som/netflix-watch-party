OTT Watch Party Extension Setup

Install CineSync in Chrome, Edge, or Brave to sync Netflix, Prime Video, or JioHotstar.

1
Open Extension Management

Open a new tab in your browser and enter the extension management URL:

chrome://extensions

(In Microsoft Edge, open: edge://extensions)

2
Enable Developer Mode & Load Extension

1. Download the CineSync extension ZIP from Google Drive.

2. Extract the downloaded ZIP file to a folder on your computer.

3. Open chrome://extensions and toggle ON "Developer mode" in the top-right corner.

4. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extracted extension folder.

Download → Extract ZIP → chrome://extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked → Select extension folder

Keep the extracted extension folder on your computer. Chrome loads the extension from this folder.

3
Start an OTT Watch Party

1. Open your provider and play a movie or show: Netflix, Prime Video, or JioHotstar.

2. Click the CineSync icon in your browser toolbar to open the Side Panel.

3. Enter the same public Sync Server URL on both computers. Do not use localhost:4000 for your friend; use the public URL shared by the host.

4. Enter your nickname and room code, then click "Join / Create Room". Share the room code or invite link with your friend.

5. Both people must be signed in to their own supported streaming account and open the same title.
